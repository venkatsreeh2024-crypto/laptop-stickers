A QUICK PRINTING NOTE

Good prints start
with good paper.

For the best result, use sticker paper suited to your printer and the surface you’re decorating. Test the size on plain paper first, and add a compatible laminate if you need extra protection.

  • ✓ SVG artwork scales without losing sharpness
  • ✓ Choose adhesive made for your surface
  • ✓ Keep wall decals and device stickers separate
